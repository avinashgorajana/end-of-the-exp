Purpose of EcoTracker The purpose of EcoTracker is to empower individuals to take control of their environmental impact by providing a user-friendly platform to track and reduce their carbon footprint. By logging daily activities such as transportation, energy consumption, and waste generation, users can gain valuable insights into their environmental habits and receive personalized recommendations for reducing their carbon footprint. With EcoTracker, users can set and achieve sustainability goals, make informed decisions about their daily habits, and contribute to a more environmentally conscious community. By promoting sustainable living and reducing carbon emissions, EcoTracker aims to make a positive impact on the environment and inspire a greener future for generations to come.
